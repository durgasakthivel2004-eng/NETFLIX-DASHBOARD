Netflix Content Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Netflix Content Analysis Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed to analyze Netflix's content library and identify patterns across countries, release years, content types, and content ratings.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of Netflix's catalog, allowing users to explore how Netflix content has evolved over time, which countries contribute the most content, and how content is distributed across different audience ratings.

The project transforms raw Netflix content data into meaningful visual insights through an easy-to-understand interactive dashboard.

🎯 Project Objective

The primary objectives of this project are to:

Analyze the geographical distribution of Netflix content.
Understand the growth of Netflix's content library over the years.
Compare the number of Movies and TV Shows released across different years.
Analyze the distribution of content based on ratings.
Identify major trends and patterns within Netflix's catalog.
Present complex data in an interactive and visually appealing dashboard.
Demonstrate the use of data visualization for business and analytical decision-making.
📊 Dashboard Features
1. Content by Country

The Content by Country visualization shows the number of Netflix titles associated with different countries.

It helps identify:

Countries contributing the highest number of titles.
The geographical concentration of Netflix content.
Differences in content production across countries.

The visualization shows the United States as the largest contributor among the countries displayed, followed by countries such as the United Kingdom, India, Spain, South Korea, Japan, Mexico, France, and Canada.

2. Release Year Trend

The Release Year Trend analyzes the number of Netflix titles based on their release year.

This visualization helps understand:

How Netflix's content catalog has grown over time.
Periods of increased content production.
Recent growth in the number of titles available on Netflix.

The trend shows relatively limited content in earlier decades, followed by significant growth in more recent years.

3. Movies and TV Shows by Year

This visualization compares the release patterns of Movies and TV Shows over time.

It helps users understand:

How the volume of Movies has changed over the years.
How TV Show releases have evolved.
Differences between Movie and TV Show growth patterns.
The period in which Netflix experienced substantial expansion of its content catalog.

The visualization indicates a strong increase in both categories during the later years represented in the dataset.

4. Rating Analysis

The Rating Analysis visualization displays the number of Netflix titles under different content ratings.

🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

🇺🇸 United States represents the largest share of content among the countries displayed.
🌍 Netflix's catalog has a strong international presence, with countries such as the UK, India, Spain, South Korea, Japan, Mexico, France, and Canada represented.
📈 Netflix content production/distribution shows substantial growth in the later years compared with earlier periods.
🎬 Both Movies and TV Shows show considerable growth over the more recent years represented in the dataset.
🔞 TV-MA is the most prominent rating category shown in the dashboard, indicating a substantial portion of the catalog is classified for mature audiences.
📺 TV-14 and TV-PG also account for significant portions of the catalog.
The dashboard demonstrates how Netflix's content library has evolved from a relatively smaller catalog into a much broader and more diverse collection.
